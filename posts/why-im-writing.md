The world is complicated right now. Here's some of what's on my mind.

**AI is changing everything.** In just a few years, AI has gone from a novelty to something that writes, codes, diagnoses and reasons alongside us. It's changing how we work, learn and make decisions, and I don't think anyone fully knows where it leads.

**America is far more violent than countries as wealthy as it.** The U.S. has the largest economy in the world. Yet its rate of deaths from assault is 7.4 per 100,000 people. That's nearly three times the average across OECD countries and at least seven times the rate in every other wealthy country in the Commonwealth Fund's comparison except New Zealand ([Commonwealth Fund](https://www.commonwealthfund.org/publications/issue-briefs/2023/jan/us-health-care-global-perspective-2022)). We have more resources than almost anyone, and we're not getting the results you'd expect.

**We're divided.** It's getting harder to talk across political lines, and easier to assume the worst about people who disagree with us.

**And if you zoom out far enough, none of it makes sense.** We're all riding on a planet that circles the Sun at about 67,000 miles per hour. Our Sun is one of hundreds of billions of stars in the Milky Way, and the Milky Way is one of hundreds of billions of galaxies. The universe is so far beyond our comprehension that it's hard to know what to do with that, except to feel small and grateful to be here at all.
